# Interview guides (45 minutes each)

Common rules: ask about the last time something happened, not what they would do. Do not pitch. Do not mention Nimikh features until the "react" block. Record only after consent (see `consent-and-privacy.md`). Two people if possible: one asks, one notes. Leave 5 minutes at the end.

Target: 5 learners, 3 instructors, 2 institute admins, 2 corporate HR (adjust after the first 3 of each).

## Hypotheses being tested (from the blueprint)
H1 Learners lose interest in long videos and accept short in-video questions. H2 A wrong answer should give feedback, not block. H3 Price and payment (bKash/Nagad/card) are barriers. H4 Employers want to verify certificates. H5 Instructors will add questions if it takes minutes. H6 Institutes need cohort reporting.

## A. Learner (current or recent online course taker)
| Min | Block | Questions |
|---|---|---|
| 0-5 | Intro and consent | Purpose, recording, they may stop anytime. Role, age band, device, data plan. |
| 5-15 | Context | Last online course you started: what, why, where (phone/laptop), when in the day? Did you finish? What made you stop? |
| 15-25 | Learning habits | How do you check you understood? Do you rewatch, take notes, skip ahead? What happens when the video is long (30+ min)? |
| 25-35 | **Key questions** | **MCQ frequency**: if a quiz pops up inside the video, how often feels right (every 3 / 5 / 10 min, only at end)? When does it annoy you? **Wrong answer**: if you answer wrong, should the video be blocked until correct, or show feedback and let you continue? Why? Would you pay for a course with required questions? **Price**: what did you last pay for a course? What is too expensive? **Payment**: how did you pay (bKash, Nagad, card, other)? Ever abandoned a payment? Why? |
| 35-42 | **Certificates** | Have you shown a certificate to an employer? Did anyone check it? Would a link or QR that proves it is real matter to you? |
| 42-45 | Close | Anything I did not ask? Who else should I talk to? Thank you gift/consent to follow-up. |

## B. Instructor (has recorded or taught an online course)
| Min | Block | Questions |
|---|---|---|
| 0-5 | Intro and consent | Subject, audience size, platforms used. |
| 5-15 | Current workflow | Walk me through publishing your last course. Tools, time, pain points. How do you know learners understood? |
| 15-25 | Assessment | Do you add questions? Where (during/after)? How long does one question take to write? Do you reuse them? |
| 25-35 | **Key questions** | **MCQ frequency**: how many questions per 10-minute video is realistic for you? **Wrong answer**: would you block progress, or only give feedback and an explanation? **Price**: how do you set your price? Revenue share you expect? **Payment**: how do you get paid today and how long does it take? Piracy/sharing worries? |
| 35-42 | Certificates | Do learners ask for certificates? Who signs them? Have fake ones been a problem? |
| 42-45 | Close | Tools you would never leave; who else to talk to. |

## C. Institute admin (training centre, school, coaching business)
| Min | Block | Questions |
|---|---|---|
| 0-5 | Intro and consent | Institute size, courses, learners per year. |
| 5-15 | Current operations | How do you run classes and track attendance/results? Which tools (Facebook groups, Zoom, Excel, LMS)? |
| 15-25 | Pain | Last time something went wrong with enrolment, payment or certificates: what happened? Hours per week spent on admin? |
| 25-35 | **Key questions** | **Price**: what do your learners pay; who sets it? **Payment**: how do you collect (bKash, Nagad, bank, cash)? Reconciliation effort? **Wrong answer / MCQ**: do your teachers want blocking or feedback? **Employer verification**: do employers or partners ask to confirm your certificates? How do you answer today? |
| 35-42 | Reporting and buying | What reports do you need monthly? Who decides to buy software? Budget range and approval steps. |
| 42-45 | Close | Willing to pilot? Contact for follow-up. |

## D. Corporate HR / L&D / hiring manager
| Min | Block | Questions |
|---|---|---|
| 0-5 | Intro and consent | Company size, hiring volume, training budget owner. |
| 5-15 | Hiring and training | How do you verify skills of candidates with online certificates? Last time you checked one: how? How long did it take? |
| 15-25 | Training | Do you assign online courses to staff? How do you know they completed it? What proof do you keep? |
| 25-35 | **Key questions** | **Employer verification**: what must a certificate page show for you to trust it (name, ID, date, issuer, revoked status)? Would you use a public link/QR? **Price**: per-seat or per-company budget? **Payment**: invoice, bank transfer, bKash/card? **MCQ / wrong answer**: for compliance training, should progress be blocked until correct? |
| 35-42 | Risks | What makes you reject a certificate? Data privacy limits for staff learning data? |
| 42-45 | Close | Pilot interest, contact. |

## After each interview (10 minutes, same day)
Fill one row in the synthesis template: top 3 quotes, surprises, which hypotheses (H1-H6) got support, contradicted, or not covered.
