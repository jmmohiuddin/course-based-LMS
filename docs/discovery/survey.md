# Survey: online learning in Bangladesh

Target: **150+ complete responses** (aim for 200 to allow for bad data). Channels: Facebook groups of students/job seekers, university clubs, institute partners, LinkedIn. Time to complete: under 4 minutes. Tool: Google Forms or similar; mobile-first. Show the consent line first (see `consent-and-privacy.md`). No incentive that attracts duplicates; one response per person (login not required, check duplicates by optional email hash).
Quota to aim for (not strict): learners 70%, instructors 15%, admins/HR 15%. Report counts by group; do not generalise from a convenience sample.

## English version
Intro: "10 short questions about how you learn online. Anonymous, about 4 minutes. Answers are used only for product research."

1. Which best describes you? (one) Student / Job seeker / Working professional / Instructor or trainer / Institute owner or admin / Employer or HR / Other
2. How often did you take an online course in the last 12 months? (one) Never / 1-2 times / 3-5 times / 6 or more
3. What device do you mostly use for learning? (one) Android phone / iPhone / Laptop or desktop / Tablet
4. If a short question pops up inside a video, how often feels right? (one) Every 3 minutes / Every 5 minutes / Every 10 minutes / Only at the end / No questions
5. When you answer a question wrong, what should happen? (one) Block until correct / Show the right answer and let me continue / Let me retry 2 times, then show the answer / Not sure
6. What is the most you would pay for one good course? (one) Free only / Up to 500 BDT / 501-1,500 BDT / 1,501-3,000 BDT / More than 3,000 BDT
7. How do you prefer to pay? (up to 2) bKash / Nagad / Rocket / Debit or credit card / Bank transfer / Cash at institute / Other
8. How important is a verifiable certificate (link or QR an employer can check)? (1 not important to 5 very important)
9. What stops you from finishing online courses? (up to 2) Videos too long / Data cost / No time / No feedback or support / Lose interest / Payment problems / Other
10. Optional: anything else we should know? (free text) Optional: your email if you accept a 30-minute interview (we store it separately).

## Analysis plan
Report n per group, percentages with counts. Q4/Q5 cross-tab by device and course frequency. Q6 as a distribution, not an average. Treat any result as supporting or weakening H1-H4, not as proof. Remove straight-liners and duplicates before counting; report how many were removed.
