# Consent and privacy

This is a practical template, not legal advice. Have it checked against Bangladesh data-protection rules and your institution's policy before use. Check participants' age: do not interview anyone under 18 without a guardian's consent.

## Consent script (read aloud or show on the form)
"Thank you for helping. I am [name] from Nimikh. We are researching how people learn online to design a course platform. This takes about [45] minutes.
- Taking part is voluntary. You can skip any question or stop at any time, with no consequence.
- With your permission I will take notes and, only if you say yes, record audio/screen. You can ask me to stop recording or delete it later.
- We will not share your name or contact with anyone outside the project team. In reports we use a code such as L3 and remove details that identify you or your employer.
- We will keep notes and recordings for [6 months], then delete them. Contact details are kept separately and deleted when the study ends or on your request.
- Your answers will not be used to sell you anything. [Thank-you gift, if any, is given regardless of answers.]
Do you have questions? Do you agree to take part? Do you agree to be recorded? (Yes / No, record the answer.)"

Bangla version: have a native speaker translate the script; do not use machine translation for consent text.

## Data handling
- Collect the minimum: role, age band, device, answers. No national ID, no payment numbers, no passwords. If a participant shows their screen with such data, pause the recording.
- Participant codes (L1, I2, A1, H1): the code-to-name list lives only in `recruiting-tracker.csv`, stored separately from notes and recordings, access limited to the research lead.
- Recordings and notes in an access-controlled shared drive (not personal phones or chat apps); phone recordings moved then deleted from the phone.
- Surveys: anonymous; the optional email field is exported to a separate sheet and removed from the analysis data.
- Delete on request within 7 days. Retention [6 months] then deletion, except anonymised quotes.
- Employer participants: do not collect confidential company data; remind them not to share names of staff or candidates.
- Prototype: stores only language, theme and demo progress in the participant's browser; no data leaves the device.
- Record each consent (yes/no, date) in the tracker.
